# Interface preferences

The initial screen is the camera interface. Do not add a landing-page hero, promotional sections, decorative photo arrangements, repeated explanations or a footer. Expose filters, adjustments, layouts and the gallery through compact controls. Layout artwork is intentionally richer than the surrounding application UI.

The owner's saved website rules apply unless a later instruction explicitly requests an exception:

1. No decorative tags or pill-shaped controls.
2. Avoid excessive italics.
3. Do not use Instrument Serif.
4. No negative letter spacing.
5. Keep the interface simple.
6. Avoid decorative or repeated dividers.
7. Use a small, consistent colour palette; avoid unnecessary shades and text opacity.
8. No bordered content containers.
9. Avoid overly detailed diagrams.
10. Keep copy concise and remove repetition.
11. No code-style breadcrumbs.
12. No section numbering.
13. No decorative eyebrow labels above headings.
14. No dithering effects.
15. No ASCII images.
16. No squircle containers.
17. Avoid excessive monospace typography.
18. No all-caps subtitles.
19. No gradient backgrounds.
20. No periods at the end of titles.

Use real, consistent icons, never emoji or text characters as icons. This application bundles Lucide icons locally. The requested typewriter and handwriting typography, paper effects, photographic borders, film effects and shadows belong to editable/exported photographic artwork, rather than general application chrome.
